Approach

Approach and sample work

The working method NorthStar intends to follow, and the practice projects behind it.

Working method

  1. Listen

    Start with the description of the problem, what has already been tried, and what a good result looks like.

  2. Assess

    Gather facts and identify the likely cause before changing anything.

  3. Plan

    Explain the options and any risk, and agree on a plan.

  4. Implement

    Make changes one step at a time, and record them.

  5. Document

    Write up what was found, what changed, and how to maintain it.

  6. Review

    Confirm the result and answer questions about it.

Skills and training

NorthStar's work draws on training and certifications in Microsoft Azure, AWS, CompTIA areas, Linux, Windows Server, Active Directory, networking, and Terraform. These are skills and credentials. They are not paid customer work or production client experience.
