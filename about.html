About ZajaTech

About ZajaTech

An IT services business built around clarity, care, and good documentation.

Purpose

Technology should support the work people do, not interrupt it. ZajaTech Solutions makes IT support, networking, and cloud administration approachable: well explained, carefully carried out, and written down.

Principles

Clarity

Findings and recommendations explained in words that support a good decision.

Care

Deliberate changes, access limited to what is needed, and security treated as part of the job.

Documentation

A record of what was done and why, so a setup stays understandable after the work ends.

Focus areas

Everyday IT help desk and device support, Microsoft 365, networking and Wi-Fi, cloud and backup on Azure and AWS, Windows and Linux servers, identity and access management, practical security configuration, and contract IT staffing.

How requests are handled

Requests are handled by an IT support professional with a healthcare support background.

Customers and capacity

ZajaTech supports households, individuals, and small businesses worldwide through remote support, and works with larger organizations on a contract basis. For larger projects, ZajaTech can expand capacity by coordinating additional IT professionals as needed.
