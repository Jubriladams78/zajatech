About NorthStar

About NorthStar

An IT services brand built around clarity, care, and good documentation.

Purpose

Technology should support the work people do, not interrupt it. NorthStar Cloud & Network Solutions is built to make IT support approachable: well explained, carefully carried out, and written down. Networking, cloud, and administration services are planned.

Principles

Clarity

Findings and recommendations explained in words that support a good decision.

Care

Deliberate changes, access limited to what is needed, and security treated as part of the job.

Documentation

A record of what was done and why, so a setup stays understandable after the work ends.

Focus areas

Everyday IT support, networks, Microsoft Azure and AWS, Linux and Windows administration, Linux servers, and Windows Server with identity administration. IT support and troubleshooting is the primary service. The other areas are planned and not yet confirmed as available; the Services page shows which.

How requests are handled

Requests are handled by an IT support professional with a healthcare support background.
